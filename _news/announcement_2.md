Graduated from Habib University with a major in Computer Science and minor in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics

---
Today marks the end of my undergraduate journey. These past four years have been full of experiences, memories, learning—and unlearning.  

Back in 2021, I began this journey as an explorative voyage into the world of science and engineering. I was curious to learn everything, which led me to take on diverse courses, projects, and research opportunities. Over time, I discovered where my true passions lie—at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics**. These fields continue to fascinate me, shaping both my academic work and future aspirations.  

Along the way, I remained committed to excellence, graduating with a **4.0 CGPA**, the **Dean’s Medal**, and the **President’s Medal**—Habib University’s highest academic honors.  

But for me, these years were never just about grades or accolades. Habib taught me the importance of **contextual awareness**, the ability to empathize, and the responsibility to use knowledge for the betterment of society. It was about listening deeply to the needs of people and thinking about how technology can serve them.  

---

### What’s Next?  
I aim to continue my journey at the crossroads of **AI and Robotics**— pushing the frontiers of embodied AI while working toward innovations that are **ethical, accessible, and impactful**. My dream is to contribute to building intelligent systems that not only push technological boundaries but also uplift communities, especially in underrepresented parts of the world.  

This chapter may be ending, but the voyage of discovery has only just begun.
